01 / ABOUT

About

I'm Syafiq Kassim, a PhD candidate in Information Technology at the School of Computing, Universiti Utara Malaysia. My research looks at how artificial intelligence can defend systems against threats such as QR code phishing, and how its decisions can be made explainable to the people who rely on them. Alongside my studies I work in IT operations, which keeps the research grounded in real users, real constraints and systems in production.

I'm registered with the Malaysia Board of Technologists as a Graduate Technologist, which recognises professional competence in applied technology and commits me to its code of conduct and continuing professional development.
