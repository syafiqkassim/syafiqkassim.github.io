About

I'm Syafiq Kassim, a PhD candidate at the School of Computing, Universiti Utara Malaysia, and an Assistant IT Officer at UUMIT, the university's IT centre. My work runs on two tracks that feed each other: day-to-day responsibility for IT services used across the campus, and doctoral research into how artificial intelligence can defend those systems, and how its decisions can be made explainable to the people who rely on them.

I'm registered with the Malaysia Board of Technologists as a Graduate Technologist, which recognises professional competence in applied technology and commits me to its code of conduct and continuing professional development.
